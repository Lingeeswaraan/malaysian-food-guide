Mee Goreng Mamak

About the dish

Mee goreng mamak is fried yellow noodles cooked the mamak style.

It's sweet, savoury and spicy at the same time. A chilli and tomato sauce gives the colour and the tangy taste.

It add on with egg, tofu, potato and vegetables.

Fun fact: It's a common late-night meal because most of the mamak stalls stay open for 23hrs.

Ingredients

  • Yellow wheat noodles
  • Egg and tofu
  • Potato and bean sprouts
  • Chilli and tomato sauce
  • Lime wedge

Quick facts

Culture: Mamak

Spicy level: Medium

Usually eaten: Lunch or dinner or supper
