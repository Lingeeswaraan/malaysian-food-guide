Milo Ice

About the dish

Milo ice is a cold chocolate drink. Milo itself is a chocolate malt powder that was first made in Australia.

It tastes sweet, chocolatey and malty.

You can order it hot or iced. Most people go for iced when the weather is hot.

Fun fact: Milo dinosaur is the same drink with a big pile of extra Milo powder on top.

Ingredients

  • Milo powder
  • Sweetened condensed milk
  • Hot water (to mix the powder)
  • Cold water or milk
  • Ice

Quick facts

Culture: Malaysian favourite from an Australia brand

Spicy level: None

Usually eaten: Any time of day
