Ais Kacang

About the dish

Ais kacang is a colourful shaved ice dessert. Ais means ice and kacang means beans. It is also called ABC.

It is icy, sweet and creamy. The syrups in bright colours and a splash of evaporated milk sweeten the ice

Fun fact: The colourful syrups often make the dessert look like a small rainbow mountain.

Ingredients

  • Shaved ice
  • Red beans
  • Sweet corn
  • Grass jelly
  • Sweet syrup and evaporated milk

Quick facts

Culture: Southeast Asian (origin debated)

Spicy level: None

Usually eaten: Afternoon dessert
