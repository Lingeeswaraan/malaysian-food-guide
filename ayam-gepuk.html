Ayam Gepuk

About the dish

Ayam gepuk is fried chicken that gets smashed with a very spicy chilli sambal. It's believed to come from Indonesia, where similar smashed chicken dishes exist.

The chicken is crispy outside and juicy inside. The sambal is hot and salty. The heat can be strong, so start with mild level if you aren't used to spiciness.

It comes with plain rice, fried tofu, tempeh, and raw vegetables like cucumber to help cool your mouth.

Fun fact: Many restaurants let you pick your spice level and there different sets to choose.

Ingredients

  • Fried chicken
  • Sambal made from chillies, garlic and shallots
  • Steamed white rice
  • Fried tofu and tempeh
  • Fresh cucumber

Quick facts

Culture: Indonesian-influenced

Spicy level: Hot

Usually eaten: Lunch or dinner
