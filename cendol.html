Cendol

About the dish

Cendol is a cold dessert with shaved ice, coconut milk and short green jelly strands.Penang is well known for this fantastic dessert.

The green strands are made from rice flour and pandan leaf. And creamy with the coconut milk, and the palm sugar on the top tastes like caramel.

It comes in a bowl or cup and you eat it with a spoon. Many places add red beans or sweet corn, and some people add durian. There's no spice at all.

Fun fact: Many Southeast Asian countries claim to have invented cendol because there is a lot of version in every country.

Ingredients

  • Shaved ice
  • Green rice-flour jelly flavoured with pandan leaf
  • Coconut milk
  • Palm sugar syrup
  • Red beans or sweet corn (optional)

Quick facts

Culture: Southeast Asian

Spicy level: None

Usually eaten: Dessert, afternoon
