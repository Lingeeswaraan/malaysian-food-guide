Mains

Stomach filling dishes you can eat for a full meal, from light coconut rice to slow-cooked rendang.

Nasi Lemak

Fine coconut rice with sweet and spicy sambal, anchovies, egg, cucumber,salty nuts.

Ayam Gepuk

Smashed fried chicken with choices of fiercy sambal.

Roti Canai

Flatbread served with dhal or curry.

Char Kuey Teow

Smoky stir-fried flat rice noodles.

Nasi Kandar

Rice with mixed Indian-Muslim curries and sides.

Laksa

Noodle soup with different style from each state to state.

Asam Pedas

Sour and spicy fish stew.

Rendang

Malay style slow-cooked spiced meat in coconut.

Mee goreng mamak

Fried noodles with cooking style of mamak.

Nasi goreng kampung

Normal fried rice but with speciality of malay village style with anchioves and special belacan sauce.
