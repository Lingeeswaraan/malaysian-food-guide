Frequently Asked Questions

What is Malaysia's national dish?

Nasi lemak is often called as the unofficial national dish

What does 'mamak' means?

Most common question asked by foreigner is this. Mamak describe as Indian-Muslim restaurant with all kind of foods including western dishes.

What is the difference between roti canai and prata?

They are very similiar flatbreads. Roti Canai is used in Malaysia, but Prata is used in India and Singapore.
