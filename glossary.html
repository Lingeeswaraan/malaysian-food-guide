Glossary

Words that you usally see in Malaysian menu which only use in Malaysia.

Kosong
Plain. Mostly people use for drink means no sugar,milk,etc..
Ais
Ice.
Panas
Hot.
Pedas
Spicy.
Manis
Sweet.
Goreng
Fried.
Lemak
Rich and creamy flavour from coconut milk.
Sambal
A chilli paste, which taste spicy and also sweet.
Kuih
Bite-sized sweet or savoury snacks.
Mamak
An Indian-Muslim eatery or stall.
Kopitiam
A traditional coffee shop.
Kampung
Village.
Ikan bilis
Dried anchovies.
