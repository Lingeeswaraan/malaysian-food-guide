Snacks & Desserts

Sweet and savoury treats that you will find almost at night markets and roadside stalls.

Apam Balik

Folded pancake with peanuts and sugar.

Satay

Grilled meat with peanut sauce.

Cendol

Iced dessert with green jelly and coconut milk.

Pisang Goreng

Crispy fried banana fritters.

Ais Kacang

Shaved ice with beans, corn and syrup.

Kuih Lapis

Colourful steamed layered cake.
