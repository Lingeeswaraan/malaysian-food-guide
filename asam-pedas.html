Asam Pedas

About the dish

Asam pedas is a sour and spicy fish stew. Asam means sour, which comes from tamarind, and pedas means spicy.

It's tangy, hot and fragrant. The sour tamarind and the hot chillies balance each other, and the herbs and a little sweetness from the tomato round it out.

It comes in a bowl with lots of gravy and you eat it with plain white rice, which soaks up the sauce. Fish like mackerel or stingray are common. It's a hot dish, and the plain rice helps with the heat.

Fun fact: Many versions use daun kesum, also called laksa leaf, which gives the stew a sharp, fresh smell.

Ingredients

  • Fish or stingray
  • Tamarind
  • Dried chillies and shallots
  • Lemongrass and daun kesum
  • Okra and tomato

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: Hot

Usually eaten: Lunch or dinner with rice
