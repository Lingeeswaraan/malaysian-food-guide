Teh Tarik

About the dish

Teh tarik means pulled tea. It's strong black tea with milk. It can be enjoyed with any meal.

It's sweet, creamy and strong, with a thick foam on top. Pouring the tea back and forth mixes in the milk.

Most people drink it with breakfast or with roti canai.

Fun fact: The tea is pulled by pouring it between two containers from a height.

  • Strong black tea
  • Sweetened condensed milk
  • Evaporated milk
  • Hot water

Quick facts

Culture: Mamak

Spicy level: None

Usually eaten: Any time with any food
