Pisang Goreng

About the dish

Pisang goreng means fried banana. You see it at roadside stalls and night marketsk.

The outside of the snack is golden and crunchy. The ripped banana inside turns soft and sweet.

The bananas usally dipped in flour for a deep-fried.

Fun fact: They go well with tea or coffee.

  • Ripe bananas
  • Flour
  • A little sugar and salt
  • Sauce

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: None

Usually eaten: Tea time snack
