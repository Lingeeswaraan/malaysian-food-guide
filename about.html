About This Guide

Malaysian Food Guide is a student website about the food that Malaysians eat every day. It covers 15 dishes, snacks and drinks, explaining what each one is, where it is believed to come from, what it tastes like and how people usually enjoy it.

All the text was written for this project. Where the history of a dish is debated, the pages say so instead of guessing. The photos come from free image sites.

Start with the mains, then try the snacks and desserts and the drinks.
