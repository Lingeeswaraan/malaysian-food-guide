Nasi Goreng Kampung

About the dish

Nasi goreng kampung means village fried rice. It's a Malay home-style dish.

The rice is savoury, a bit spicy and smells great. The fried anchovies add crunchines to the rice.

It's often eaten for breakfast or dinner with a fried egg on top and fried chicken.

Fun fact: A very hot wok gives the rice a smoky smell.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Dried anchovies
  • Chillies, garlic and shallots
  • Water spinach
  • Fried egg

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: Medium

Usually eaten: Breakfast, lunch or dinner
