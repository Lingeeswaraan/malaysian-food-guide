Extra Joss

About the dish

Extra Joss is an energy drink powder that comes in small sachets. It's an Indonesian brand that is also sold widely in Malaysia. You mix it with water to drink it.

It has a sweet, fruity taste, and most people drink it cold with ice. The flavour depends on the variety.

Because it comes in single sachets, it's light and easy to carry around. Most stalls sell it as a mixed iced drink.

Fun fact: I recommend trying Anggur (Grape) flavour, it's loved by many.

Ingredients

  • One sachet of Extra Joss powder
  • Cold water
  • Ice

Quick facts

Culture: Indonesian brand, widely sold in Malaysia

Spicy level: None

Usually drunk: Any time
