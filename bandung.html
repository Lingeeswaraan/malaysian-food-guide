Bandung

About the dish

Bandung is a pink colorish drink made with rose syrup and milk.

It tastes sweet and creamy, with a light flowery taste from the rose. It's best when its iced.

You'll often see it at wedding feasts, Ramadan bazaars and mamak stalls. Some people add basil seeds.

Fun fact: The pink colour comes from the rose syrup and some people use actual rose petals.

Ingredients

  • Rose syrup
  • Milk
  • Sweetened condensed milk
  • Ice
  • Basil Seed (optional)

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: None

Usually drunk: Weddings, Ramadan bazaars and hot days
