Satay

About the dish

Satay is meat on skewers that is marinated and grilled over charcoal. It's same as BBQ but different in the sauce.

The meat comes out smoky and juicy with a slightly sweet taste.

Chicken, beef and mutton are the usual choices. You get the skewers with peanut sauce, cucumber and raw onion.

Fun fact: The cook keeps fanning the charcoal while turning the skewers, so you can often smell a satay stall before you see it.

  • Chicken, beef or mutton
  • Turmeric, lemongrass and spices for the marinade
  • Peanut sauce
  • Cucumber and onion

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: Mild to medium

Usually eaten: Dinner or snack
