Nasi Kandar

About the dish

Nasi kandar is steamed rice served with your choice of curries and side dishes. It's a Mamak dish that's strongly linked to Penang.

It tastes rich, spicy and savoury. The meats (fish, chicken, beef) and vegetable curries mix together on the rice.

You pick your sides at the counter and the waiter pours curry over the rice.

Fun fact: Kuah campur nasi kandar is so special that they mix all the currys and kicaps on the rice.

Ingredients

  • Steamed white rice
  • Any meat curry
  • Fried chicken or fried fish
  • Vegetables
  • Boiled or fried egg/li>

Quick facts

Culture: Mamak

Spicy level: None to hot

Usually eaten: Lunch or dinner
