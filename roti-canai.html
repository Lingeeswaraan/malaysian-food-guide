Roti Canai

About the dish

Roti canai is a soft flatbread and it's one of the most popular meals at mamak restaurants.

The outside is crispier and the inside is soft.

Mostly the flavour comes from dhal or chicken curry and my favourite is the fish curry. It often drink with teh tarik.

Fun fact: The cook spins and flips the dough in the air to stretch it paper thin, which is fun to watch.

  • Flour
  • Water and a pinch of salt
  • Ghee or oil
  • Dhal or curry for dipping

Quick facts

Culture: Mamak

Spicy level: Bread is mild, curry spiciness varies

Usually eaten: Breakfast or supper
