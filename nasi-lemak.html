Nasi Lemak

About the dish

Nasi lemak is coconut milk rice and many people call it Malaysia's unofficial national dish.

The sambal is sweet, salty and spicy so it blend through the creamy rice. The anchovies and peanuts add crunchiness.

It's traditionally a breakfast dish, served on a plate or wrapped in banana leaf.

Fun fact: You can add on fried chicken, boiled egg or fried egg.

Ingredients

  • Rice cooked with coconut milk and pandan leaf
  • Sambal
  • Fried anchovies and roasted peanuts
  • Cucumber slices
  • Boiled or fried egg/li>

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: Medium

Usually eaten: Breakfast
