Kuih Lapis

About the dish

Kuih lapis is a steamed cake made of thin layers. Malay and Peranakan communities both make it, and it's found around the region, so we don't really know its exact origin.

It's soft, chewy and lightly sweet and the coconut milk makes it a little creamy.

The finished cake is cut into pieces and sold at markets and breakfast stalls.

Fun fact: Peeling the layers apart one by one is the traditional way to eat it, and it's half the fun.

Ingredients

  • Rice flour and tapioca starch
  • Coconut milk
  • Sugar
  • Food colouring, often red and white or pandan green

Quick facts

Culture: Malay

Spicy level: None

Usually eaten: Breakfast or tea time
